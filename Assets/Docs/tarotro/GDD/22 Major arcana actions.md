A full compendium of all 22 Major Arcana adapted for the three-card spread mechanic: **Past** (Base Action/Effect), **Present** (Power/Modifier), and **Future** (Final Multiplier).

Since the player can spend **Will** to invert cards, each Arcanum has two completely different incarnations.

## Block 1: From Chaos to Order (0 — V)

### 0. The Fool

- **Upright (Absolute Chaos):**
    
    - _Past:_ Chooses a random base action (Attack 15 or Armor 15).
        
    - _Present:_ Adds a random value to Power (from +1 to +25).
        
    - _Future:_ Gives a random multiplier (from x0.5 to x3.5).
        
- **Reversed (Madness):**
    
    - _Past:_ Deals 5 pure damage to the hero, but draws 2 cards from the deck.
        
    - _Present:_ Mirror: swaps the current values of Base Power and Multiplier.
        
    - _Future:_ All or nothing: gives either x0 or x5 (50/50 chance).
        

### I. The Magician

- **Upright (Elemental Mastery):**
    
    - _Past:_ Elemental attack (12 damage) + applies Burn to the enemy.
        
    - _Present:_ Doubles the base Power value of the card in the "Past" position.
        
    - _Future:_ Adds +0.5x to the multiplier for each Ring worn.
        
- **Reversed (Forbidden Sorcery):**
    
    - _Past:_ Applies "Vulnerability" to the enemy (+50% incoming damage for 2 turns).
        
    - _Present:_ Converts all of the hero's current Armor into damage and adds it to the turn's power.
        
    - _Future:_ Locks the final multiplier at x2.0, but returns 1 Will point.
        

### II. The High Priestess

- **Upright (Secret Knowledge):**
    
    - _Past:_ Pure Armor (15 units).
        
    - _Present:_ Meditation: restores 1 Will point.
        
    - _Future:_ If the player's hand has 0 cards left, gives a powerful x3.0 multiplier.
        
- **Reversed (Hidden Sins):**
    
    - _Past:_ Evil Eye: steals 10 units of Armor from the opponent.
        
    - _Present:_ Distortion: instantly turns all cards in hand into their reversed versions.
        
    - _Future:_ Prophecy: the current turn deals no damage, but doubles the power of the next spread.
        

### III. The Empress

- **Upright (Fertility and Life):**
    
    - _Past:_ Blessing: Heal (8 HP) + Armor (8 units).
        
    - _Present:_ Adds +5 to the hero's maximum health until the end of combat.
        
    - _Future:_ Multiplier equals (Hero's current HP / 10).
        
- **Reversed (Nature's Tyranny):**
    
    - _Past:_ Explosive Overgrowth: deals damage equal to the hero's current Armor value.
        
    - _Present:_ Desecration: removes all positive buffs from the target.
        
    - _Future:_ Blood Harvest: sacrifices 10 hero HP, but gives a fixed x3.5 multiplier.
        

### IV. The Emperor

- **Upright (Iron Will):**
    
    - _Past:_ Heavy Blow (14 damage) + Armor (6 units).
        
    - _Present:_ Command: sets the current turn's minimum Power threshold at 20.
        
    - _Future:_ Gives a x2.5 multiplier if the hero has maximum shield active.
        
- **Reversed (Despotism):**
    
    - _Past:_ Armor Break: Attack (10 damage) that completely ignores the enemy's defense.
        
    - _Present:_ Demoralization: reduces the opponent's base attack by 50% on the next turn.
        
    - _Future:_ Tax: blocks the Discard mechanic on the next turn, but gives x4.0 now.
        

### V. The Hierophant

- **Upright (Sacred Dogma):**
    
    - _Past:_ Ritual: applies "Sanctity" (the enemy's next hit deals 0 damage).
        
    - _Present:_ Orthodoxy: gives +6 Power for each Upright card in the current spread.
        
    - _Future:_ Stable x2.2 multiplier (cannot be reduced by debuffs).
        
- **Reversed (Heresy):**
    
    - _Past:_ Anathema: curses the enemy (they lose HP every time the player discards cards).
        
    - _Present:_ Apostasy: gives +12 Power if the entire spread consists of Reversed cards.
        
    - _Future:_ The Fall: reduces the Multiplier to x1.0, but doubles the gold earned from the battle.
        

## Block 2: Trials of Fate (VI — XI)

### VI. The Lovers

- **Upright (Union and Synergy):**
    
    - _Past:_ Kindred Souls: links two enemies (damage to one is duplicated to the other).
        
    - _Present:_ Mimicry: fully copies the base effect of the card in the "Past" position.
        
    - _Future:_ Duality: gives x2.0. If the spread contains "The Emperor" or "The Empress," the multiplier becomes x4.0.
        
- **Reversed (Discord):**
    
    - _Past:_ Sacrificial Bond: deals 12 units of damage to both the enemy and the hero.
        
    - _Present:_ Split: halves the current turn's Power, but strikes twice instead of once.
        
    - _Future:_ Betrayal: forces a random minion to attack its own boss.
        

### VII. The Chariot

- **Upright (Triumph and Speed):**
    
    - _Past:_ Lightning Lunge (10 damage). Draws +1 card into hand on the next turn.
        
    - _Present:_ Momentum: makes the card in the "Future" position trigger twice.
        
    - _Future:_ Onslaught: gives a x1.8 multiplier, and this turn doesn't consume the time/rage gauge.
        
- **Reversed (Crash/Hubris):**
    
    - _Past:_ Ram: stuns the enemy, pushing their attack intention back by 1 turn.
        
    - _Present:_ Recklessness: gives +25 Power, but deprives the player of the draw phase on the next turn.
        
    - _Future:_ Wreck: deals massive base damage (35), but breaks one random Ring until the end of combat.
        

### VIII. Strength

- **Upright (Fortitude of Spirit):**
    
    - _Past:_ Furious Strike (16 damage).
        
    - _Present:_ Overcoming: adds to Power a value equal to the hero's lost HP.
        
    - _Future:_ Long-term trigger: gives +0.2x to the final multiplier for each turn of combat that has passed.
        
- **Reversed (Raw Rage):**
    
    - _Past:_ Chokehold: blocks the enemy's ability to apply debuffs for 1 turn.
        
    - _Present:_ Suppression: reduces the power of the target's next attack by a fixed 15 units.
        
    - _Future:_ Brute Force: gives a colossal x4.5 multiplier, but if the enemy survives, it performs an immediate counterattack.
        

### IX. The Hermit

- **Upright (Search for Truth):**
    
    - _Past:_ Solitude: restores +1 Discard charge.
        
    - _Present:_ Frugality: gives +2 gold for each card remaining in hand after assembling the spread.
        
    - _Future:_ Isolation: gives a x3.5 multiplier, but only if the player's hand has no cards left.
        
- **Reversed (Exile):**
    
    - _Past:_ Memory Wipe: permanently removes a chosen card from hand until the end of the current battle.
        
    - _Present:_ Paranoia: deals damage equal to the amount of gold in the player's purse (max 30).
        
    - _Future:_ Decay: consumes the entire discard pile, turning each card in it into +3 Power.
        

### X. Wheel of Fortune

- **Upright (Blessing of Luck):**
    
    - _Past:_ Jackpot: gives a random effect (Attack 30, Shield 30, or +50 gold). Equal chances.
        
    - _Present:_ Spin the Reel: adds a random number from 0 to +33 to Power.
        
    - _Future:_ Risk: the multiplier becomes either x0 or x4.0.
        
- **Reversed (Doom):**
    
    - _Past:_ Fatalism: takes away 50% of the current health of either the enemy or the hero.
        
    - _Present:_ Burning Bridges: freezes your gold and converts it into Power this turn (1 to 1).
        
    - _Future:_ Inversion of Fate: temporarily reverses all Rings worn, activating their shadow properties.
        

### XI. Justice

- **Upright (Karma and Balance):**
    
    - _Past:_ An Eye for an Eye: deals damage exactly equal to the damage the hero took on the previous turn.
        
    - _Present:_ Equalization: makes the hero's Armor value equal to the enemy's Armor value.
        
    - _Future:_ Retribution: gives a x3.0 multiplier if the hero's current HP is lower than the target's.
        
- **Reversed (Bias):**
    
    - _Past:_ Blinding: reduces the enemy's hit chance by 50% on the next turn.
        
    - _Present:_ Corruption: takes all negative statuses (poisons, curses) off the hero and applies them to the enemy.
        
    - _Future:_ Distortion of Judgment: inverts the enemy's attack on the next turn — their strike will heal the hero.
        

## Block 3: Death and Destruction (XII — XVI)

### XII. The Hanged Man

- **Upright (Sacrifice for the Future):**
    
    - _Past:_ Enlightenment: the hero completely skips the attack phase this turn, but gains +2 Will points.
        
    - _Present:_ Stasis: locks the current cards on the table in place — they don't go to the discard pile and remain for the next turn.
        
    - _Future:_ Time Loop: this turn's damage is delayed, but dealt threefold (x3.0) after 1 turn.
        
- **Reversed (Useless Sacrifice):**
    
    - _Past:_ Martyrdom: deals 5 pure damage to the hero, but poisons the enemy with deadly venom.
        
    - _Present:_ Apathy: resets all of the opponent's accumulated buffs and rage multipliers.
        
    - _Future:_ Self-Centeredness: gives an insane x5.0 multiplier, but stuns the hero for the entire next turn.
        

### XIII. Death

- **Upright (The Great Cleansing):**
    
    - _Past:_ Harvest (15 damage). If this hit finishes off the enemy, the hero restores 100% HP.
        
    - _Present:_ Necrosis: destroys (burns) the card in the "Past" position, permanently adding its power to the deck.
        
    - _Future:_ Scythe: gives a pure, uncompromising x3.0 multiplier.
        
- **Reversed (Rebirth from the Ashes):**
    
    - _Past:_ Necromancy: returns a random Major Arcana card from the discard pile straight into hand.
        
    - _Present:_ Decomposition: deals damage that scales with the number of cards in the discard pile.
        
    - _Future:_ Reincarnation: the multiplier is fixed at x1.0, but fully refreshes all Discard and Will charges.
        

### XIV. Temperance

- **Upright (The Golden Mean):**
    
    - _Past:_ Healing of the Soul: restores 12 units of health.
        
    - _Present:_ Harmony: copies the effect of the card on the left, reducing its base power by only 10%.
        
    - _Future:_ Purification: clears all debuffs from the hero and locks the multiplier at a stable x2.0.
        
- **Reversed (Distortion of Proportions):**
    
    - _Past:_ Alchemy: instantly converts all of the enemy's accumulated Armor into gold for the hero (10 to 1).
        
    - _Present:_ Imbalance: steals the rage gauge from the opponent and transfers it to the character.
        
    - _Future:_ Excess: steals an attack buff from the enemy, multiplies it by x1.5, and applies it to the current turn.
        

### XV. The Devil

- **Upright (Temptation of Power):**
    
    - _Past:_ Hellfire: deals 25 units of damage, but sets the hero on fire (2 damage per turn).
        
    - _Present:_ Vicious Contract: adds +50 Power, but shuffles a dummy "Obsession" card into the deck.
        
    - _Future:_ Temptation: grants a massive dark x5.0 multiplier.
        
- **Reversed (Breaking the Chains):**
    
    - _Past:_ Subjugation of Will: forces the enemy to skip a turn or attack its own minions.
        
    - _Present:_ Sabotage: blocks the opponent's passive effects and rings from acting for 2 turns.
        
    - _Future:_ Emancipation: gives a x6.66 multiplier, but in the next battle the player will have one fewer slot in the spread.
        

### XVI. The Tower

- **Upright (Sudden Collapse):**
    
    - _Past:_ Cataclysm: deals 35 damage and completely destroys (zeroes out) the enemy's Armor.
        
    - _Present:_ Collapse: zeroes out all of the hero's current shields, converting every 2 units of armor into +5 Power.
        
    - _Future:_ Chaos: gives a x4.0 multiplier, but completely destroys the spread (cards cannot be returned by discarding).
        
- **Reversed (Prolonged Crisis):**
    
    - _Past:_ Earthquake: stuns all enemies on the battlefield, interrupting their current casts.
        
    - _Present:_ Ruins: deals damage equal to 200% of the player's Armor value, breaking the shield.
        
    - _Future:_ Clean Slate: gives a x0 multiplier, but grants the hero absolute immortality until the start of the next turn.
        

## Block 4: Cosmic Triumph (XVII — XXI)

### XVII. The Star

- **Upright (Hope and Progress):**
    
    - _Past:_ Starlight: applies a passive buff (gives +5 Armor at the end of every turn until the end of combat).
        
    - _Present:_ Constellation: every Upright card played in this spread permanently gains +2 base power.
        
    - _Future:_ Astrology: gives a x1.5 multiplier + 0.5x for each "Star" played during the entire current run.
        
- **Reversed (Lost Direction):**
    
    - _Past:_ Eclipse: blinds enemies, causing their attacks to miss 30% of the time.
        
    - _Present:_ Shadow Compass: converts all of the hero's current debuffs into pure Power.
        
    - _Future:_ Disillusionment: gives a high x3.5 multiplier, but all other cards in hand lose 2 units of base power.
        

### XVIII. The Moon

- **Upright (Illusions and Secrets):**
    
    - _Past:_ Phantom Blade (12 damage). This strike cannot be blocked or dodged.
        
    - _Present:_ Deception: reverses the card in the "Future" position for free, without spending Will points.
        
    - _Future:_ Murk: hides the final multiplier behind a question mark. It is calculated randomly in the range x1.5 to x4.5.
        
- **Reversed (Unveiling Fears):**
    
    - _Past:_ Nightmare: makes the enemy panic, dealing damage to itself equal to its own base attack.
        
    - _Present:_ Sleepwalking: makes all reversed cards in hand immune to enemy curses.
        
    - _Future:_ Ebb Tide: gives a multiplier equal to the number of all Reversed cards in the player's deck.
        

### XIX. The Sun

- **Upright (Triumph and Clarity):**
    
    - _Past:_ Cleansing Flame: deals 20 damage and completely removes ALL negative effects from the hero.
        
    - _Present:_ Zenith: adds +20 Power. If the hero is at 100% health, the bonus increases to +40.
        
    - _Future:_ Dawn: a powerful, pure x3.5 multiplier.
        
- **Reversed (Scorching):**
    
    - _Past:_ Sunburn: deals 10 damage to the enemy and blinds it for 1 turn.
        
    - _Present:_ Drought: reduces the Will cost of reversing cards to 0 until the end of the current turn.
        
    - _Future:_ Sunset: burns the enemy's discard pile, depriving it of special resurrection mechanics, but gives a modest x1.8 multiplier.
        

### XX. Judgment

- **Upright (Awakening of the Spirit):**
    
    - _Past:_ Redemption: returns one randomly burned card from this battle back into the deck.
        
    - _Present:_ Verdict: equates the current turn's Power to the hero's Maximum health value.
        
    - _Future:_ Apocalypse: gives a x3.0 multiplier, but only if "Judgment" is the last card played from hand.
        
- **Reversed (Inevitable Punishment):**
    
    - _Past:_ Reckoning: deals damage to the enemy equal to the total HP the hero has lost over the entire run.
        
    - _Present:_ Rebirth of the World: completely replaces the player's current deck with 22 random reversed cards.
        
    - _Future:_ Cancellation of the Verdict: completely resets the current turn and restarts the battle while keeping all gold.
        

### XXI. The World

- **Upright (Absolute Perfection):**
    
    - _Past:_ Harmony of the Universe: simultaneously deals 20 damage, gives 20 armor, and heals 20 HP.
        
    - _Present:_ Integration: instantly sums up and activates the passive effects of ALL worn Rings at once.
        
    - _Future:_ Final Chord: gives the maximum base multiplier x5.0.
        
- **Reversed (Stagnation / Void):**
    
    - _Past:_ Cosmic Vacuum: completely erases and cancels the enemies' intentions for this turn.
        
    - _Present:_ Polarization: doubles the numerical effects of all Upright cards in the spread and completely nullifies the Reversed ones.
        
    - _Future:_ Möbius Loop: returns the entire current spread back to the player's hand, ending the turn without discarding cards.
